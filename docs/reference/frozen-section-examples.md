# General conventions observed

1. **Specimen lettering is retained through every section.** Diagnosis lines begin with `(A)`, `(B)`, etc.; labels may be upper-case organ/site names, followed by `excision x frozen section - NO EVIDENCE OF MALIGNANCY`. This exact `x`/hyphen convention was requested for LIS saving.
2. **Preferred final-paraffin opener:**

   > The paraffin sections confirm the frozen section diagnosis.

   This occurs in the user-supplied preferred tongue and SLN formats, and in the lung wedge example (plural “sections”).
3. **Negative nodes include the numerator/denominator** where established: `NO EVIDENCE OF MALIGNANCY (0/2)` or `(0/1)`.
4. **Gross handling convention** records fresh receipt, measurement, whether material was bisected/serially sectioned, number of frozen blocks, and subsequent paraffin processing: e.g.,

   > All embedded in 1 frozen block, and subsequently converted to paraffin block.

   or

   > All embedded in one frozen block, subsequently processed to paraffin.
5. **Communication timestamps may be documented**: received time, slides-dispatched time, phone-report time, fax-report time; a concise alternative is:

   > The frozen section result was phone reported to Dr. Chang at 1207 on 05/08/2026.

6. **Concordance vs discordance is explicit.** Concordance can be written as:

   > The permanent section findings on both right SLN1 and SLN2 are concordant with the intraoperative frozen section impression, both of which were negative for malignancy and phone reported at 1150 on 05/08/2026.

   The discordant-node model begins:

   > The initial frozen section impression of atypical cells in specimen A was discordant with the final diagnosis.

7. **No documented “Deferred to paraffin” or “favour” house phrasing** was found in the retrieved concrete reports.
