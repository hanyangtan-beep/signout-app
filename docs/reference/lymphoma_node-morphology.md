# Lymph node for lymphoma: classification, morphology, immunohistochemistry and ancillary studies for a reporting template

*Specimens: lymph node excision, excisional and incisional biopsy, and core biopsy, examined in house or received as slides for review. Compiled 14 October 2026. British spelling; quotations keep the original spelling. "n.a." = not confirmed from a fetched source.*

## 1. Sources and scope

- **Classification.** WHO Classification of Haematolymphoid Tumours, 5th edition (WHO-HAEM5), summarised for lymphoid neoplasms by Alaggio et al., *Leukemia* 2022 ([PMC9214472](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214472/)). The form uses the WHO-HAEM5 entity names.
- **The other 2022 scheme.** International Consensus Classification (ICC), Campo et al., *Blood* 2022 ([PMC9479027](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9479027/)). Where the ICC differs on a point the form touches, both are given (§8).
- **Cell of origin.** Hans et al., *Blood* 2004 ([PubMed 14504078](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14504078/)), with the 30% read-out as summarised on PathologyOutlines ([MUM1](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmum1.html)) and the Canadian harmonisation study ([PMC13317902](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13317902/)).
- **Entity pages.** PathologyOutlines: [DLBCL, NOS](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomadiffuse.html), [follicular lymphoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomafollicularnodalusual.html), [classic Hodgkin lymphoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomanonBclassic.html), [nodal marginal zone lymphoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomanodalMZL.html), [Kikuchi-Fujimoto disease](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphnodeskikuchi.html). The mantle cell, CLL/SLL, angioimmunoblastic and NLPHL pages could not be read (robots.txt); for those entities the WHO-HAEM5 text is used.
- **Hodgkin grading.** BNLI grade of nodular sclerosis, from the review of classic Hodgkin lymphoma in *Mediterr J Hematol Infect Dis* ([PMC4063617](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4063617/)).
- **Out of scope.** Extranodal lymphoma (stomach, skin, CNS, marrow), cytology, flow cytometry reporting and staging. Lymphoma is not staged by AJCC TNM, so the form has no pTNM.

The Kikuchi wording follows the PathologyOutlines sample report, "Necrotizing lymphadenitis (see comment)", with the comment that the findings favour Kikuchi disease ([PathologyOutlines](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphnodeskikuchi.html)).

## 3. Follicular lymphoma: grade, centroblast count and pattern

- **Grading is optional in WHO-HAEM5.** "In WHO-HAEM5, grading of FL, which is only pertinent to cFL, is no longer mandatory." The reason given is poor reproducibility and questionable clinical significance ([WHO-HAEM5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214472/)).
- **The ICC keeps it.** "For follicular lymphoma (FL), the consensus was to retain morphologic grading (grades 1-2, 3A, and 3B) according to previously described criteria" ([ICC 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9479027/)).
- **Counts (PathologyOutlines table).** Grade 1: "0 - 5 centroblasts / high power field"; grade 2: "6 - 15 centroblasts / high power field"; grade 3A: "> 15 centroblasts / high power field Centrocytes present"; FLBL (3B): "> 15 centroblasts / high power field Lacks centrocytes". "High power field of 0.159 mm2 (40x objective)" ([PathologyOutlines FL](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomafollicularnodalusual.html)).
- **Diffuse areas.** For grade 3A: "If diffuse component: reported as diffuse large B cell lymphoma and follicular lymphoma (% of each component is reported)" ([PathologyOutlines FL](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomafollicularnodalusual.html)).
- **Pattern words.** The form offers follicular, follicular and diffuse, and predominantly diffuse. The percentage bands of the 2017 classification are **n.a.** (not in a fetched source) and are not printed.
- **House wording (biopsy export, aggregate).** The released lines print the grade in lower case after the entity ("FOLLICULAR LYMPHOMA, grade 3A"), name a DLBCL component after it ("with focal DIFFUSE LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA"), and give the count as "Centroblasts are counted at N/HPF" with the field definition in brackets. Each phrase occurs in fewer than 10 released diagnoses.

## 4. Classic Hodgkin lymphoma

- **Immunophenotype.** In the PathologyOutlines sample report the large abnormal cells "are positive for PAX5 (weak), CD30 and CD15 (subset) and are negative for CD20, CD45 and CD3"; CD15 is positive in "75 - 80% of cases" ([PathologyOutlines cHL](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomanonBclassic.html)).
- **BNLI grade of nodular sclerosis.** "The term grade II is applied to cases showing one of the three following patterns: 1. more than 25% of the nodules have a cellular composition consistent with the pleomorphic or reticular subtype of LD-CHL; 2. more than 80% of the nodules display a fibrotic or fibro-histiocytic composition; 3. more than 25% of the nodules contain numerous large bizarre or anaplastic cells, in the absence of any depletion of the reactive small lymphoid component" ([PMC4063617](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4063617/)). House wording prints the grade in capitals with "(BNLI)" after it, in fewer than 10 released diagnoses.

## 5. Large B-cell lymphoma: cell of origin, MYC and BCL2

- **Cell of origin stays.** "It is recommended to continue rendering the GCB/ABC (GCB/nonGCB) distinction although it has become apparent that the clinical impact of COO stratification is relatively limited outside clinical trials" ([WHO-HAEM5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214472/)).
- **Hans algorithm.** It uses CD10, BCL6 and MUM1 ([Hans 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14504078/)). With CD10 negative and BCL6 positive, MUM1 "> 30%" gives the non-germinal centre type and "< 30%" the germinal centre type; CD10 is negative when it is in fewer than 30% of the malignant cells ([PathologyOutlines MUM1](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmum1.html)). The Canadian study adjusted the read-out to "a cutoff of >30% of >2+ staining intensity" for sensitive protocols ([PMC13317902](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13317902/)). The form takes each marker as positive or negative as read; the 30% cut-off sits in the field help and the Checks.
- **Dual expression.** "Double expresser status: immunohistochemical coexpression of MYC (≥ 40% tumor cells) and BCL2 (≥ 50% tumor cells)" ([PathologyOutlines DLBCL](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lymphomadiffuse.html)); the same cut-offs, "≥ 40%, ≥ 50%", in [PMC11262692](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11262692/).
- **Double hit.** The WHO-HAEM4R entity "has been conceptually reframed and reassigned" as DLBCL/HGBL with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements. Cases with MYC and BCL6 rearrangements "have been excluded from the DLBCL/HGBL-MYC/BCL2 entity and are now classified either as a subtype of DLBCL, NOS or HGBL, NOS according to their cytomorphological features" ([WHO-HAEM5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214472/)).
- **House wording (biopsy export, aggregate).** Cell of origin prints as "non-GCB type" or "GCB type" after the entity, and the microscopy names the method ("by Hans classifier", "(Hans algorithm)"). In this form the method moves to the Comment, the user's rule for methods.

## 7. Slides received for review

- **House wording (biopsy export, aggregate).** Referral cases print "for slide review" in the specimen line, with the referring number, the slide count and the hospital in brackets. The microscopy opens with the slides received ("H&E sections, immunostained sections, in situ hybridisation sections and paraffin blocks are received from …") and what they represent. "Slide review" appears in about a third of the released lymphoma diagnoses in the biopsy file.
- **Guideline.** No fetched source sets the wording of a referral report: **n.a.**

## 8. Where the sources disagree

| Point | WHO-HAEM5 | ICC 2022 | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| FL grading | Optional for cFL | Retained (1-2, 3A, 3B) | Grade is a field with "Not graded"; FLBL is its own entity |
| NLPHL | NLPHL kept; "may be more accurately called nodular lymphocyte predominant B-cell lymphoma" | "Nodular lymphocyte predominant B-cell lymphoma" replaces the term | WHO-HAEM5 term |
| Dual expressor | Not an entity | "should be deemphasized" | Not in the diagnosis line; the percentages print in the microscopy, and Checks and a comment snippet name it |
| MYC/BCL6 double hit | DLBCL, NOS or HGBL, NOS | n.a. (not confirmed from the fetched ICC text) | A Check, wording from WHO-HAEM5 |

## 9. What prints and what stays in the Checks

- **Prints.** The specimen and referral details, the morphology, the immunophenotype and Ki-67, EBER, the centroblast count when typed, FISH and clonality in MOLECULAR FINDINGS, the WHO-HAEM5 entity with grade, subtype or cell of origin, and in the Comment the flow cytometry correlation and the cell-of-origin method.
- **Checks only.** The grade cut-offs, the dual-expressor cut-offs, the Hans read-out, the Burkitt Ki-67 anchor, the cHL and nTFHL markers, and the FISH-based reclassification. The report register (`docs/new-template-brief.md` §3.2) keeps thresholds, citations and exclusion logic out of the printed text.
