# Gastric biopsy: polyps, adenomas, carcinoma types and biomarkers

Research notes for the `gastric_biopsy` template, Phase 57. Compiled 7 October 2026.

**How to read this document**
- Each statement links to the page it was taken from. The WHO blue book (6th edition, 2026) was not read; its changes come from two published reviews.
- Nothing here prints in a released report. The released wording is in `docs/specs/gastric-biopsy-polyps-carcinoma.md`, and the criteria sit in the schema help text and reference panels.
- Copies of the QMH pages are in `docs/reference/Biomarker-Stomach.html`, `Biomarker-Stomach-HER2.html` and `Biomarker-PD-L1-IHC.html`, with a text dump in `docs/reference/qmh/stomach-biomarkers.txt`.

## 1. Polyps and adenomas

- **Hyperplastic polyp:** elongated, architecturally distorted pits with cystic dilatation, foveolar epithelium with an apical mucin cap, an oedematous lamina propria and smooth muscle strands ([Pathology Outlines, hyperplastic polyp](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachhyperplastic.html)). "Elongated, tortuous, and cystically dilated foveolar glands within an edematous and inflamed lamina propria" ([Gastric Adenomas and Mimickers, Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)). Dysplasia involves the surface and begins abruptly, while regenerative change matures toward the surface ([Pathology Outlines](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachhyperplastic.html)).
- **Fundic gland polyp:** cystically dilated glands lined by chief cells, parietal cells and mucinous foveolar cells; hyperplastic parietal cells with apocrine snouting in patients on proton-pump inhibitors; sporadic polyps rarely harbour dysplasia, and FAP-associated polyps are more likely to show low grade dysplasia ([Pathology Outlines, fundic gland polyp](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachfundicgland.html); [Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)).
- **Intestinal-type adenoma:** dysplastic columnar epithelium with goblet and Paneth cells and no apical mucin cap; the background may show chronic gastritis with intestinal metaplasia ([Pathology Outlines, adenomas](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachadenomas.html)). Diffuse MUC2 and CDX2 with apical CD10 ([Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)).
- **Foveolar-type adenoma:** dysplastic foveolar epithelium with apical mucin caps; the background mucosa is usually without inflammation, atrophy or metaplasia; hybrid intestinal and gastric phenotypes occur ([Pathology Outlines, adenomas](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachadenomas.html)). Diffuse MUC5AC ([Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)).
- By convention, gastric adenomas show at least low grade dysplasia ([Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)). This is the basis of the check on an intestinal-type or foveolar-type adenoma without a grade.
- **Pyloric gland adenoma:** closely packed tubular glands lined by low cuboidal to columnar cells with eosinophilic, ground-glass cytoplasm; it occurs without dysplasia, with low or high grade dysplasia, or with adenocarcinoma ([Pathology Outlines, pyloric gland adenoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachpyloricglandadenoma.html)). No apical mucin cap; diffuse MUC6, with MUC5AC mostly on the surface ([Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)).
- **Oxyntic gland adenoma:** dilated, branched, fused and anastomosing glands in the lower oxyntic mucosa, made mainly of chief cells with basophilic cytoplasm and no more than mild nuclear atypia; the surface may be spared; submucosal invasion makes it an adenocarcinoma of fundic-gland type ([Pathology Outlines, oxyntic gland adenoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachoxynticglandadenoma.html)). Diffuse pepsinogen I and MUC6 ([Diagnostics 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297954/)). In biopsies, diffuse strong cyclin D1 helps separate it from normal oxyntic glands ([WHO 6th edition review, Histopathology 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13128328/)).
- **Inflammatory fibroid polyp:** submucosal spindle and stellate cells with onion-skin cuffing of thin-walled vessels, an oedematous stroma rich in eosinophils and minimal mitotic activity ([Pathology Outlines, inflammatory fibroid polyp](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stomachinflammatoryfibroid.html)); CD34 positive and c-kit negative ([StatPearls, gastric polyps](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560704/)).

**WHO 6th edition.** The sections on gastric dysplasia, intestinal-type adenomas and gastric-type adenomas are combined under gastric dysplasia, mirroring MAPS III; pyloric gland adenomas and oxyntic gland neoplasms are kept apart ([Histopathology 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13128328/)). The template keeps the adenoma terms because the user asked for them.

## 2. Carcinoma types

- WHO 6th edition (2026): the major histological categories are largely unchanged; poorly cohesive carcinoma is split into signet-ring cell and non-signet-ring cell types; carcinoma with lymphoid stroma, AFP-producing carcinoma, micropapillary adenocarcinoma, adenocarcinoma of fundic-gland type and sarcomatoid carcinoma are named among the uncommon types ([Virchows Archiv 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00428-026-04697-7); [IARC, Digestive System Tumours](https://publications.iarc.who.int/662)). Gastroblastoma has moved to the mesenchymal chapter ([Histopathology 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13128328/)), so it is not offered.
- The CAP stomach protocol lists tubular, poorly cohesive, mucinous, papillary and mixed adenocarcinoma, then hepatoid, carcinoma with lymphoid stroma, micropapillary, fundic-gland type, squamous, adenosquamous, undifferentiated, large cell and small cell neuroendocrine carcinoma and the mixed neuroendocrine neoplasms ([CAP Stomach 4.4.0.0](https://www.cap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Stomach_4.4.0.0.REL_CAPCP-1.pdf?download=true)).
- Grade by gland formation: well (more than 95% glands), moderately (50 to 95%) and poorly differentiated (49% or less) ([CAP Stomach](https://www.cap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Stomach_4.4.0.0.REL_CAPCP-1.pdf?download=true)). The template offers a grade for tubular and papillary adenocarcinoma, and for adenocarcinoma whose subtype is not assessable.
- Lauren: intestinal, diffuse (includes signet-ring cell carcinoma) and mixed (approximately equal amounts of intestinal and diffuse) types; the Lauren classification may be used with the WHO system ([CAP Stomach](https://www.cap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Stomach_4.4.0.0.REL_CAPCP-1.pdf?download=true)).
- Carcinoma with lymphoid stroma: syncytial growth with poorly formed glands, dense tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes and no desmoplasia; most are EBV-associated, and EBV-associated carcinoma is defined by EBER in the tumour cells ([EBV-associated gastric carcinoma review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3140657/)).
- The morphology sentences for the other types are the biopsy counterparts of the resection sentences in `generators/gastric_morphology.js`. They describe architecture and cells only, without stromal, depth or percentage claims that a biopsy cannot support.

## 3. Biomarkers

**QMH page.** The Stomach/OGJ biomarker page (updated 11 April 2025) orders HER2 IHC (biopsy and excision), HER2 DISH, EBER ISH, MMR IHC, MSI, PD-L1, claudin 18.2, then cold ischaemia and fixation times ([QMH Stomach biomarkers](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-Stomach.html); [QMH templates](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/)). The printed order follows it (test: "biomarker order follows the QMH stomach biomarker page").

**HER2.**
- Biopsy scoring uses a cancer-cell cluster of at least 5 cells, whatever the percentage stained: 0 and 1+ are negative, 2+ equivocal and 3+ positive; resections use 10% of cancer cells ([CAP gastric HER2 template](https://documents.cap.org/protocols/cp-biomarker-gastric-her2-2017-v1000.pdf); [QMH HER2](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-Stomach-HER2.html)).
- ISH: amplification is a HER2/CEP17 ratio of 2 or more; the template records the average HER2 signals per cell and the ratio ([CAP gastric HER2](https://documents.cap.org/protocols/cp-biomarker-gastric-her2-2017-v1000.pdf)).
- ISH is obligatory for an equivocal 2+ result ([Biomarker review 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13109289/)).

**EBER.** QMH offers negative or positive in the tumour cells, cannot be determined, and "reactivation in the lymphoid cells" ([QMH Stomach biomarkers](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-Stomach.html)). The template's "positive in background lymphoid cells only" maps to that last option.

**PD-L1.**
- CPS is the number of PD-L1-staining tumour cells, lymphocytes and macrophages, divided by the viable tumour cells, times 100; gastric PD-L1 expression is CPS 1 or more ([22C3 interpretation manual](https://www.agilent.com/cs/library/usermanuals/public/29276_22C3_pharmdx_uc_interpretation_manual_us.pdf)).
- CPS is reported as an absolute value from 0 to 100. The review advises against a bare "positive" or "negative" because therapies use different cut-offs: pembrolizumab CPS 10 or more, nivolumab CPS 5 or more, tislelizumab tumour area positivity 5% or more ([Biomarker review 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13109289/)).
- In May 2025 the FDA limited pembrolizumab and nivolumab in HER2-negative gastric adenocarcinoma to CPS 1 or more ([CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-limits-pembrolizumab-nivolumab-in-pd-l1-low-gastric-cancer-indications)).
- QMH groups at CPS 10 and lists the 22C3 (Dako) and E1L3N (Cell Signaling) antibodies, with TPS and CPS fields ([QMH PD-L1](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-PD-L1-IHC.html); [QMH Stomach biomarkers](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-Stomach.html)).
- The template therefore prints the CPS and its clone without a label (a reversible choice).

**Claudin 18.2.**
- Positive is 75% or more of viable tumour cells with moderate to strong membranous staining. Cytoplasmic staining is not scored, and at least 50 viable tumour cells are needed ([Roche VENTANA CLDN18 (43-14A) interpretation guide](https://diagnostics.roche.com/content/dam/diagnostics/us/en/products/v/ventana-cldn18-(43-14a)-rxdx-assay/cldn18-43-14a-rxdx-assay-interpretation-guide.pdf)).
- The guide describes the intensities but does not number them. QMH records 2+ and 3+ percentages and their total ([QMH Stomach biomarkers](https://awiloqmh.github.io/CancerReportingTemplate/Biomarker-Stomach.html)), and the 2026 review states the cut-off as 2+/3+ in 75% of tumour cells, with normal gastric mucosa as the internal positive control ([Biomarker review 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13109289/)).
- Zolbetuximab was approved in October 2024 for CLDN18.2-positive, HER2-negative gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma, with the VENTANA CLDN18 (43-14A) RxDx assay as the test ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zolbetuximab-clzb-chemotherapy-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma)).

**Not carried.** QMH's MSI fragment analysis and the cold ischaemia and fixation times are not carried in the biopsy template.
