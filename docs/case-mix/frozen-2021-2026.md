# Frozen section case mix, 1 January 2021 to 14 September 2026

Source: `210101-260914 FS anonymised.xlsx`, 598 frozen section cases, one row each, with the
reported Gross, Frozen (intraoperative), Micro and Diagnosis text in full. Single responsible
pathologist, single test code. The file is already anonymised; it stays in the sandbox and only
the aggregate counts and the deidentified report phrasing below leave it.

Parts were recovered from the diagnosis head lines (`(A) ORGAN, detail, procedure:`), giving
**1291 specimen parts across 598 cases** — a mean of 2.2 parts per frozen case, which is itself
the first finding: the frozen template's multi-part path is the normal path, not the exception.
70 cases (11.7 per cent) use a head line format the parser did not recognise; see *Two registers*
below, which is why.

### What this changes

1. **Margins are the template, not a scenario.** Two of every five parts are a margin, and the
   descriptor vocabulary is small and repeating: deep (12), posterior (11), anterior (10), medial
   (9), lateral (8), inferior (7), superior (5), lateral deep (5), deep resection (7), proximal
   resection (3), posterior deep (3), anterior deep (3), right/left lateral, right superior,
   o'clock positions, stump, marrow, ureteric, staple line, transection. These belong in a pick
   list with the organ, not typed each time.
2. **The sentinel node scenario is mostly not breast.** 236 sentinel parts against 25 breast
   parts in the whole file. The numbering convention is fixed — `right SLN1`, `left SLN 2`,
   `left SLN 3` — and the same case routinely carries SLN1 through SLN3 as separate letters. The
   template's `Breast sentinel lymph node` scenario should generalise to a sentinel node scenario
   with a site, an auto-numbered series and the breast pairing as one case of it.
3. **CNS and pituitary are the largest uncovered category** — 48 parts, 8.0 per cent of cases,
   with a short and formulaic answer list: meningioma (11 printed lines), pituitary adenoma,
   high-grade glioma, schwannoma, epidermoid cyst, infarcted tissue with adjacent brain. There is
   no CNS scenario today and no squash or smear vocabulary.
4. **Urological margins** (ureteric, urethral) are 13 parts — real but small, and served
   adequately by a generalised named-margin list rather than their own scenario.

**The node-count suffix `(0/1)` is a house convention the template does not print.** 102 cases
use it. It is the involved-over-examined count appended in parentheses to the node diagnosis line,
and it should be generated from the node fields rather than typed.

23 cases print a `pTNM` stage line in a frozen report — all lung wedges (`Stage pT1b (pTNM, AJCC
8th edition)`), consistent with the wedge being the definitive resection.

## Intraoperative result lines

The `Frozen` block is a signing pathologist line, the result per part, and a phone sentence.
Result vocabulary, in order: `No malignancy` (138), `Negative for malignancy` (122),
`Adenocarcinoma` (37), `No evidence of malignancy` (15), `Meningioma` (9), `Carcinoma present` (6),
`Metastatic carcinoma` (7), `Spindle cell tumour` (3), `Metastatic adenocarcinoma` (3),
`At least mucinous borderline tumour` (3), `Inadequate for diagnosis` (2), `Scanty suspicious
cells present` (2), `Atypical cells present - need immunostain to confirm`, `Carcinoma; need
immunostaining for further subtyping`, `Adenocarcinoma, staple line margin involved`.

Three phrasings of the same negative answer — `No malignancy`, `Negative for malignancy`,
`No evidence of malignancy` — account for 275 of roughly 400 result lines. The template should
offer one and let the other two be reachable, not leave the choice to whatever was typed last.

**Letter grouping is already house style**: `(A)-(D) No malignancy.` appears throughout, which the
template's `labelRange` behaviour matches.

## Standard sentences to lock down

Block sentence, 900 occurrences across six variants that differ only in numerals and a comma:

- `All embedded in 1 frozen block, and subsequently converted to paraffin block.` (480)
- `All embedded in one frozen block, and subsequently converted to paraffin block.` (199)
- `All embedded in 2 frozen blocks, and subsequently converted to paraffin blocks.` (95)
- `All embedded in one frozen block and subsequently converted to paraffin block.` (49, no comma)
- `All embedded in 1 frozen block and subsequently converted to paraffin block.` (45, no comma)
- `All embedded in one block for frozen section.` (32, not converted)

Digit versus word and the serial comma are the only differences. One generated sentence removes
the variation. 518 cases (86.6 per cent) carry the conversion clause.

Phone sentence, 515 cases (86.1 per cent):

- `The frozen section result was phone reported at HHMM on DD/MM/YYYY.` (129)
- `The frozen section result was phone reported to Dr <name> at HHMM on DD/MM/YYYY.` (112 name the
  surgeon)
- a minority write the time as `HH:MM` (9) or the year in two digits (6)

Gross opener: `Received in a fresh state is/are …` is universal.

## Two registers

The file contains two house styles that never merged over five years:

- **Uppercase register** — `TONGUE, anterior, excision for frozen section:` with
  `- NO EVIDENCE OF MALIGNANCY.` This is the majority and is what the template generates today.
- **Sentence-case register** — `Lymph node, right axillary sentinel LN, biopsy:` with
  `- No evidence of malignancy.`, often a tab-indented dash (37 cases). 24 cases use the
  sentence-case negative line; 70 cases carry a head line in this register.

The sentence-case cases cluster in the sentinel node and peritoneal/omental work. This is worth a
deliberate decision rather than drift: one register, generated.

## Paraffin correlation

- 328 cases (54.8 per cent) open the microscopy with a confirmation sentence — `Paraffin sections
  confirm frozen section diagnosis…`, which the template already generates.
- 3 cases mention deferral.
- **No case in five years uses a discordance word** (discordant, discrepant, revised, on review of
  the frozen). Either discordance is not recorded in this field, or it is genuinely rare. The
  template's discordance-reason field has no support in this data and should not be made
  prominent on the strength of an assumption.

## Proposed ordering for the frozen template

On measured volume, not on judgement:

1. **Named margin pick-lists with the organ** — 40.4 per cent of parts, and the single largest
   thing the template still leaves as free text.
2. **Generalise the sentinel node scenario** — 18.3 per cent, presently reachable only through the
   breast scenario. Site, auto-numbered SLN1..n, the `(0/1)` count generated.
3. **Node-count suffix on the diagnosis line** — 102 cases, small change, immediate.
4. **One block sentence and one phone sentence, generated** — removes six and four variants
   respectively across 900 and 515 cases.
5. **CNS / pituitary scenario** — 8.0 per cent of cases, entirely uncovered, short answer list.
6. **Result-line vocabulary per scenario** — replace the guessed options with the measured ones.
7. **Register decision** — uppercase or sentence case, applied by the generator.

Items 1 to 4 are changes to an existing template with existing fixtures. Item 5 is a new scenario.
