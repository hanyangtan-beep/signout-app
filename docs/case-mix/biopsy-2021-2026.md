number, no site text, no patient field, and no case text is quoted anywhere in this document.

## Structural findings

**The two biggest templates parse nothing from a pasted gross.** `gastric_biopsy` and
`colonic_polyp` have an empty `macroExtract`, and together they are 38 per cent of all parts.

**The biopsy gross is as formulaic as the frozen one, and differently worded.** Across 15,003 gross
part-chunks: the formalin opener in 92.9 per cent, "All embedded" in 82.2, a dimension in 76.0,
a colour word in 75.9, a consistency word in 59.6, a cassette count in 55.8, a piece count in 50.1,
an uncountable "fragments" in 8.8, a size range written "A to B" in 11.5. Pieces, dimension and
cassette count are all three present in 49.9 per cent. None of this vocabulary is in
`src/macroparse.js`, which was written for resections and retuned for frozens.

**Parts are not created from the paste.** 2,735 cases (29 per cent) have two or more parts, up to
twelve. The gross letters agree with the diagnosis letters in 97 per cent of those cases, so the
gross alone is a reliable part list; the comma-separated site list agrees in only 62 per cent, so
it is a good source of names but a poor source of counts.

**A numbered block key is already written in 8.7 per cent of grosses**, in the same "(1) …. (2) …."
form the app's block key block prints.

**Hedging is house register, not an exception.** Of 15,025 diagnosis lines: "in keeping with" 387,
"suggestive of" 290, "consistent with" 248, "compatible with" 61, "favour" 39 — 6.8 per cent
overall. Templates carry these phrases ad hoc, differently in each generator.

**The gastric separator question is settled by the corpus.** Where a gastritis line carries a second
finding, the house writes a semicolon: 388 lines with one, none without.

**One idea the data refuted.** Micro register does not track piece count: "The section shows" is
used in 13.1 per cent of single-piece single-cassette cases and 11.9 per cent of the rest. The
singular opener is a personal habit, not a rule, so the app should keep writing "Sections show".

## Proposed work, in order

1. **Gastric semicolon fix** (S) — one line. The corpus settles it 388 to 0.
2. **Biopsy macro vocabulary in `src/macroparse.js`** (M) — piece count including "fragments",
   aggregate size and "A to B" ranges, cassette count, colour, consistency, embedding statement;
   then `macroExtract` on `gastric_biopsy`, `colonic_polyp` and the other biopsy schemas. Same shape
   as the frozen work, roughly ten times the caseload behind it.
3. **Parts created from the paste** (M) — a lettered gross creates parts A…N, names them from the
   site list when the counts agree, and leaves the template picker to the user.
4. **Block key from the paste** (S) — fill the block key block when the gross already numbers it.
5. **Shared certainty qualifier** (S) — one control emitting the house hedges, replacing the ad hoc
   phrases in individual generators.
6. **Oral cavity biopsy template** (M) — 223 parts, the largest uncovered category, and already
   queued as a frozen and synoptic pair.
7. **Bone and marrow biopsy template** (M) — 245 parts, of which only the 59 odontogenic lines are
   covered by `jaw_cyst`.
