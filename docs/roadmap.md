- 2026-09-19 — Ependymal tumours are classified by anatomical compartment first (user: "shouldnt the ependymoma algorithm be location based first?"). They were right and the pathway was wrong: it asked ZFTA of every ependymoma, which is a supratentorial question, and had no route at all to YAP1, MYCN or the surrogate immunopanel. WHO CNS5 classifies these tumours by anatomical site and then by the molecular group of that site (cIMPACT-NOW update 7, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8018155/), so the stage `ep` now asks morphology, pattern and compartment before any test, and hands off to one of four: `epm` for the two types made on morphology alone — subependymoma and myxopapillary ependymoma leave before the compartment is ever asked, with HOXB13 where the morphology overlaps a spinal ependymoma; `epst` for ZFTA then YAP1, with L1CAM and p65 standing in where fusion testing is out of reach; `eppf` for H3 K27me3, EZHIP and methylation, with the NOS route spelled out when all three are unrevealing; and `epsp` for the mimics, MYCN and copy number, with no grade assigned once MYCN is amplified. Seven new Layer 4 fields, all gated on `molStatus`, and `h3k27me3` re-gated off the IDH branch so an ependymoma can reach it at all. The checks follow the compartment rather than the entity name: a ZFTA fusion in the posterior fossa is NEC, PFB without methylation is a warning, high-grade spinal histology with no MYCN is a prompt, and a diagnosis whose compartment disagrees with the recorded site is flagged. The guided intake asks each compartment only its own questions. Writing the tests turned up a real defect in shipped code: `/fusion detected/` also matches "No fusion detected", so a negative ZFTA or BRAF fusion had been reading as a positive one — now a single predicate across the three modules, with a regression test. Trial gains a supratentorial ZFTA and a spinal MYCN starting point. 1096 tests, release gate green. Spec `docs/specs/cns-ependymal-algorithm.md`.
