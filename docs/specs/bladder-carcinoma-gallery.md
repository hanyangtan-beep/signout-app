# Urinary Bladder Carcinoma Cystectomy — Reference Galleries (`bladder_carcinoma`)

Five side-drawer reference panels attached to `select` / `multiselect` fields in `schemas/bladder_carcinoma.json` (QMH `UG-Urinary Bladder`, CAP Urinary Bladder 4.2.0.0, WHO 5th edition 2022, AJCC 8th edition Chapter 62).

## Panels

1. **`histType` — Urothelial carcinoma subtypes & divergent differentiation (WHO 5th)**
   - 6 groups, 6 photomicrographs + ExpertPath links (`https://app.expertpath.com/`).
   - Covers non-invasive papillary & conventional invasive UC, micropapillary UC, plasmacytoid UC, nested UC, sarcomatoid UC, and clear cell (glycogen-rich) UC & divergent differentiation.
2. **`grade` — WHO 5th grading of urothelial & non-urothelial bladder carcinomas**
   - 2 groups, 1 photomicrograph + ExpertPath links.
3. **`cisStatus` — Urothelial carcinoma in situ (UCIS) vs benign / reactive urothelium**
   - 2 groups, 4 photomicrographs + ExpertPath links.
   - Covers UCIS H&E, aberrant full-thickness CK20, aberrant p53 overexpression, and benign urothelium with superficial umbrella cells.
4. **`extension` — Urinary bladder staging landmarks (pT1–pT4b)**
   - 4 groups, criteria + ExpertPath links.
   - Covers lamina propria/muscularis mucosae (pT1) vs muscularis propria (pT2a/pT2b), perivesical fat (pT3a/pT3b), prostatic stroma transmural (pT4a) vs urethral/ductal pitfall, and adjacent organs / pelvic wall (pT4a/pT4b).
5. **`marginStatus` — Cystectomy surgical margins (ureteral, urethral, soft tissue)**
   - 2 groups, criteria + ExpertPath links.
