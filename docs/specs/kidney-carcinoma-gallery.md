# Renal Cell Carcinoma Nephrectomy — Reference Galleries (`kidney_carcinoma`)

Five side-drawer reference panels attached to `select` / `multiselect` fields in `schemas/kidney_carcinoma.json` (QMH `UG-Kidney`, CAP Kidney 4.3.0.0, WHO 5th edition 2022, AJCC 8th edition Chapter 60).

## Panels

1. **`histType` — Renal cell tumour subtypes (WHO 5th edition)**
   - 5 groups, 6 photomicrographs + ExpertPath links (`https://app.expertpath.com/`).
   - Covers clear cell RCC, papillary RCC, chromophobe RCC (classic & eosinophilic) vs renal oncocytoma, clear cell papillary renal cell tumour, and molecularly defined / high-grade RCC (TFE3/TFEB, FH-deficient, SDH-deficient, collecting duct, SMARCB1-deficient medullary).
2. **`grade` — WHO/ISUP nucleolar grading (Grades 1–4)**
   - 4 groups, 2 photomicrographs + ExpertPath links.
   - Covers G1–G2 (400x), G3 (100x), G4 (pleomorphism / sarcomatoid / rhabdoid), and tumours not graded by WHO/ISUP (chromophobe RCC, clear cell papillary RCT, multilocular cystic renal neoplasm of LMP).
3. **`sarcomatoid` — Sarcomatoid and rhabdoid differentiation**
   - 2 groups, criteria + ExpertPath links.
4. **`extension` — pT3a–pT4 anatomic landmarks (renal sinus, fat, vein, adrenal)**
   - 3 groups, criteria + ExpertPath links.
   - Highlights renal sinus fat/vein (pT3a), perinephric fat & IVC levels (pT3a/b/c), and contiguous adrenal (pT4) vs non-contiguous ipsilateral adrenal metastasis (pM1).
5. **`marginStatus` — Nephrectomy surgical margins (partial vs radical)**
   - 2 groups, criteria + ExpertPath links.
